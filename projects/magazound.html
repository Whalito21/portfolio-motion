The brief

Magazound was the final project for our Sponsorship Management course: an 11-person team had to plan, fund and run a real event. My classmates voted me Project Manager, and I co-led the project with a co-PM.

We built a free fashion × music festival around one line: "Take it slow, don't go fast (fashion) and (music)."

The day

  1. TalkFast fashion

    A guest speaker on the cost of fast fashion.

  2. RunwayUpcycled fashion show

    Students modelled outfits made entirely from used fabric.

  3. SunsetLive music

    TU Folksong, then Tofu, Playing Saliva and Rungaroon.

  4. All dayFood, drinks & art

    Booths from an open call.

What I did

  • Split the 11-person team into 7 departments on day one and gave each one its tasks
  • Planned the work schedule and meetings, then, with my co-PM, tracked every department's progress for 65 days, up to show day
  • Kept spending in line with the budget, together with my co-PM
  • Helped build the 30-post PR plan across Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, as a team
  • Liaised with artists and sponsors and made the final calls
  • Made part of the artwork, and ran the post-event review with my co-PM to find what to fix next time

The result

  • ~600attendees at a free event
  • 8 + 4financial sponsors, plus media partners Fungjai, Spacebar Vibe, Mehey and Fuzz Bros
  • No lossthe project closed without a loss, and the post-event survey came back positive

TU Folksong goes back to the 1980s. Since 2004, members join by audition and are placed in "houses" named after musical symbols. The club's stage is Larn Folk, a concert series run entirely by its members.

The audition is open to students from any university. I auditioned in my second year and got in as a bassist.
